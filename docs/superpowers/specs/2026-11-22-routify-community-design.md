# Routify community: on the road, shared, Plus unlocks more

## Why
People come back to Routify during the trip, not only while planning, because collecting gems on the
road is fun. Every finished trip becomes a card people share, which brings new travellers for free.
Free users get a basic community; Plus unlocks the rest.

## Goals
- Retention: something to do at every gem on the road.
- Free promotion: a share card per trip, and invite links that reward both sides.
- Plus conversion: the best parts of the community are Plus.

## Non-goals and anti-goals
- No fake users, bots or invented posts (misleading under EU consumer law, and against Routify's
  "checked, not invented" promise). Launch content is real and labelled (Routify team, testers).
- No reward for an unverifiable "share" (it could be faked endlessly); rewards follow real sign-ups.

## What a traveller does
1. **Check in at a gem**: within 300 m (location checked) tap "I'm here": a stamp in the trip passport and points.
2. **Photo challenge** at the gem (exists): extra points; the photo goes to the feed after a check.
3. **Trip passport**: stamps of this trip ("4 of 9 gems").
4. **Share card** at the end: route, gems, km, countries, Routify name; an image for a story, with the
   traveller's personal invite link.

## Invites (decided with Jarne)
- A friend who signs up through someone's invite link gets **2 weeks of Plus free** (no card).
- The inviter gets **1 week of Plus** per friend who signs up (at most 8 weeks a year).

## Free and Plus
| | Free | Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Check-ins, stamps, passport, share card, invite link | yes | yes |
| See and like photos | yes | yes |
| Post photos | 1 a day | unlimited |
| Points | normal | double |
| Explorer gems (rare places found by travellers, turned official) | no | yes |
| Suggest a new gem, name on it when official | no | yes |
| Monthly challenge with a prize | no | yes |
| Badges | basic | all |

## Launch content (honest)
- A "Routify team" account posts real gem photos (own photos, or open-licence photos with credit), labelled as the team.
- Testers of the soft launch post real photos.
- "Gems of the week" from the gem library fills the feed from day one.
- Launch challenge: the first 100 approved photos give a month of Plus.

## Approaches considered
- **Points-only game** (no Plus split): simplest, but gives no reason to pay. Not chosen.
- **Social network** (follow people, comments, profiles): heavy to build and moderate. Not now.
- **Chosen: trip-based collecting** (check-ins, passport, share card, invites) on top of what exists, with a Plus split.
