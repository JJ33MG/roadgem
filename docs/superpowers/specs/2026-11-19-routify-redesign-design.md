# Routify: redesign and gems along the route

Date: 2026-11-19 · Status: waiting for Jarne's approval

## Intent and why

RoadGem (renamed **Routify**) plans a European road trip in seconds: the days, a bed every night, a rental car
for the trip, and "road gems", places worth a short stop along the way. It earns from a subscription (Free and
Plus, €9/month) and affiliate links (Booking.com, Airbnb, rental cars, Viator, GetYourGuide).

The concept works, but the current site looks generated by AI (a dark startup template, orange plus purple
accents, pill labels, emoji icons) and the gems are not really along the route. Jarne will not launch it like
that. The goal is a site that looks professionally designed, works for every age, and makes the gems and a
community game the reason people come back between trips, so it can go live and start earning soon.

## Goals

1. A new look built with the Hallmark design method, theme **Ribbon** (dawn colourway): calm, light, Instrument
   Sans over Figtree, one teal accent, machined controls. Chosen by Jarne from three previews.
2. Usable by all ages: body text 18px or more, controls at least 48px tall, strong contrast, plain words.
3. Trip planning with many filters that stay compact:
   - Main panel: From, To, days, **kilometres a day** (slider), **gems a day** (slider), a **"Road gems on the
     way" switch** (on by default; off plans only the route and the stays), **sleep in** (campsites, hotels,
     Airbnb and holiday homes).
   - A row of the most-used filters (family, friends, old towns, nature, step-free) and an **All filters** sheet
     for the rest (pace, budget, pets, EV charging, longest detour for a gem, and more).
4. **Gems really along the route**: a few around the start city, a few around the end city, and gems spaced
   about every 50 to 100 km along the driving route, each with its detour time ("+12 min"), mixed by type and
   weighted by the traveller's interests.
5. **Overnight stops by distance**: each day ends near the chosen kilometres, at a place with stays of the
   chosen types, with booking links (affiliate).
6. **Community game**:
   - Travellers post photos of gems they visited (real photos alongside Google's).
   - Travellers suggest new gems with a photo and a pin; after enough visits and likes from others a suggestion
     becomes an official gem, the finder's name stays on it.
   - Official gems carry a **photo challenge** (for example "both towers of the city hall in one shot"); a photo
     taken on the spot earns points. The photo's location is checked against the gem.
   - A **feed** of posts others can like, and an **explorers board** ranking travellers by points.
7. Works on laptop and phone from one website, installable on the phone's home screen (a PWA).
8. A preview at a real web address that Jarne can test before anything goes public.

## Non-goals (for now)

- A native App Store or Google Play app (later, once the site earns).
- Gem alerts while the app is closed (needs the native app).
- Stripe payments going live (still waiting for company verification; the existing direct upgrade stays for testing).
- Multiple languages (English first, as today).

## Anti-goals (a failure even if it works)

- Anything that reads as AI-made: eyebrow labels, dot-joined meta strings, emoji icons, gradient washes,
  invented numbers, testimonials or logos.
- Filters that take over the screen.
- Points that can be earned from home (no location check), or unmoderated photos going public.

## Constraints

- Existing stack stays: React 18 + Vite + Tailwind (frontend), Express + Prisma + PostgreSQL (backend), Claude
  for itineraries, Google Places for places and photos, Tavily for research.
- Hallmark's Floor rules hold on every page; its sloplint check runs before each preview.
- Existing routes and data keep working: auth, trips, saved trips, subscription, affiliates, community gems.
- The Railway project "renewed-growth" already has the database, Redis and every API key.

## Approaches considered

| | What | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **A, picked** | One responsive website that installs as a phone app (PWA) | One codebase on what exists, live in weeks, camera and location work; no gem alerts while closed |
| B | A now, a native app later | Adds alerts when closed and store presence, but a second build, $99/year and store reviews; the later step |
| C | Native app first | Much slower to first users, and long trips are planned on a big screen; dropped |

Jarne picked A, with B once the site earns.

## How it is built (decided)

**Phases, each ending in a preview Jarne tests:**

1. **Look and planning** — new design system (Ribbon tokens, components), new homepage, trip planner panel with
   the new filters, trip results page (days, map, stays per night, rental car, gems), login and sign-up, pricing,
   PWA install. Uses the current trip generation, with kilometres a day and the gems switch passed through.
2. **Gems along the route** — the real engine: take the driving route from Google Directions, sample points
   along it, search Google Places (and free OpenStreetMap and Wikipedia data) near those points, measure each
   candidate's detour, keep the best mix per stretch, plus start and end city gems. Overnight places chosen near
   each day's kilometre target.
3. **Community** — photo posts on gems, new-gem suggestions with the official-gem threshold, photo challenges
   with on-the-spot location checks, the feed with likes, the explorers board, moderation (an AI check like the
   current community gems, then admin approval for anything public).

**Preview:** a separate "preview" environment in the Railway project, deployed from this branch, so the live
address stays untouched until Jarne approves.

## Left to the builder (my calls, adjustable)

- Points: photo at an official gem 10, completed challenge 50, suggested gem that turns official 200.
- Official-gem threshold: 10 distinct visitors with a location-checked photo and at least 20 likes.
- Location check: within 300 m of the gem when the photo is taken in the app.
- Default trip settings: 350 km a day, 3 gems a day, gems on, campsites and hotels.
- Photos stored in object storage on Railway; component structure, data models and API shapes as the plan defines.
- Brand: the wordmark "Routify" set in Instrument Sans 500; no logo mark until a real one is designed.
